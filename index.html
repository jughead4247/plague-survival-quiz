How Would You Survive a Plague Outbreak?

Think you could survive a deadly plague outbreak? The Ultimate Plague Survival Test is a fictional survival quiz where your decisions determine how well you would handle the collapse of normal society.

Face 40 difficult outbreak decisions involving infection control, food, water, medicine, shelter, evacuation, resource management, other survivors, leadership, and long-term survival.

Your decisions determine your Survival Score. Some decisions also reveal something about your values and contribute to a separate Morality Score.

What You'll Face in the Plague Survival Test

The 40-decision survival test puts you in situations where every choice can affect your chances of surviving.

  • Protecting yourself from infection
  • Managing food and water
  • Finding safe shelter
  • Deciding when medical treatment is worth the risk
  • Managing limited resources
  • Helping or refusing other survivors
  • Making difficult moral decisions
  • Building a long-term survival strategy

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the plague survival quiz work?

You face a series of fictional plague-outbreak situations. Each situation gives you four choices. Your decisions affect your survival result.

How many questions are in the plague survival test?

The test contains 40 decisions. Each decision presents four choices. Every choice contributes to Survival Score, while selected decisions also contribute to Morality Score.

What is the Survival Score?

Your Survival Score represents how effectively your choices handle infection risk, resources, shelter, medical needs, travel, and other survival challenges.

What is the Morality Score?

Morality is measured only on decisions where your choice actually reveals something about your values. It can reflect compassion, fairness, responsibility, loyalty, sacrifice, justice, and respect for human life.

Is this a real survival test?

No. This is fictional entertainment and does not predict how someone would perform during a real disaster, epidemic, or emergency.

Can I take the test again?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after receiving your result and make different decisions.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. Use the Challenge Your Friends button after completing the test and see who makes the best survival decisions.
